# Quick Reference: 0-3 Redressability Scoring

Use the same 0-3 scale for all eight dimensions.

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Absent: no usable evidence. |
| 1 | Nominal: generic or internal-only support. |
| 2 | Procedurally usable but incomplete. |
| 3 | Institutionally actionable: specific, evidence-backed, and tied to a usable process or responsible owner. |

## Eight dimensions

1. Detectability: can the affected person or reviewer know an action occurred?
2. Traceability: can the action be traced to evidence, tools, policies, and actors?
3. Contestability: is there a concrete route to challenge the action?
4. Reversibility: is there a concrete rollback/correction path?
5. Remedy: are repair, correction, compensation, rescheduling, or equivalent remedy options visible?
6. Recurrence prevention: is there a visible path to prevent repeated failure?
7. Burden allocation: does the institution preserve evidence and reduce the affected person's burden?
8. Value-chain assignability: can responsibility be assigned across model, agent, tool, deployer, and human requester/supervisor?

Score only what is visible. Do not give credit for a process that you assume exists but cannot see.
